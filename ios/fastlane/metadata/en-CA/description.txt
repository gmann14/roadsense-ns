RoadSense NS turns everyday drives into a public map of potholes and rough road conditions across Nova Scotia.

While you drive, RoadSense measures road roughness using your phone's motion and location sensors. Your readings combine with everyone else's to build a community road-quality map that can help drivers, residents, and decision-makers see which roads need attention.

No account. No sign-up. Open the app, allow location and motion access, and drive normally.

What you get:

- A public road-quality map for Nova Scotia
- Passive drive collection while your phone is locked or in your pocket
- One-tap pothole reports, with optional photos while stopped
- Personal stats showing kilometres mapped and contributions made
- Privacy controls for places you do not want included

Privacy you can verify:

- RoadSense trims sensitive trip endpoints before upload
- You can add privacy zones for home, work, or other frequent stops
- No account is required
- Device tokens rotate periodically
- You can delete local app data from Settings
- Background location is used only for road-quality collection

Road quality is usually measured with expensive equipment on long cycles. RoadSense puts a lightweight version of that public-interest data collection in every driver's pocket and makes the result visible to the community.

Built in Halifax. Free and ad-free.
