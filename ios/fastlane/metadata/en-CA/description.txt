RoadSense NS turns everyday drives into a public map of rough roads and potholes across Nova Scotia.

While you drive, RoadSense measures how rough the road is using your phone's motion and location sensors. Your readings are combined with other drivers' to build a community road-quality map that helps residents, journalists, and decision-makers see which roads need attention.

No account. No sign-up. No ads. Allow location and motion access, start driving, and RoadSense does the rest.

WHAT YOU GET

- A public road-quality map for Nova Scotia, from smooth to very rough
- Automatic drive detection: collection starts and stops on its own while your phone stays in a mount, cupholder, or pocket
- Hands-free pothole reports: say "Hey Siri, mark a pothole in RoadSense", or tap the button when you're a passenger or stopped
- Your own stats: kilometres mapped, drives, and contributions
- Privacy zones for home, work, or anywhere you don't want included

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

- Privacy zones and drive start/end trimming run on your phone, so readings near the places you protect are never uploaded
- No account, no name, no email required
- Uploads use a random device token that rotates regularly and is never tied to your Apple ID
- The public map only shows a road once at least three different contributors have driven it, and public dates are rounded to the day
- Raw readings are deleted on a fixed schedule
- You can delete your local data any time in Settings

DRIVE SAFELY

RoadSense is designed to run without any interaction while you drive. Never handle your phone while driving. Use Siri, or leave pothole reporting to a passenger.

WHY

Road condition data is usually collected with specialised vehicles on long cycles. RoadSense puts a lightweight version of that public-interest measurement in every driver's pocket and makes the results visible to everyone at nsroadsense.ca.
